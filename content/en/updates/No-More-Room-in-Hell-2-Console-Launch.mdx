## Weekly Assignments and Merit Farming Guide

The **Weekly Assignments** system is the fastest way to accumulate **Merits**, which unlock the most exclusive **Cosmetics**. Each week, four assignments rotate, and they scale in difficulty. Understanding how to optimize your playtime around these assignments is key to maximizing your progression.

### Assignment Categories

Assignments fall into three categories: kill-based, objective-based, and survival-based. Kill-based assignments are straightforward—eliminate X number of **Walkers** or **Crawlers** with specific weapons. Objective-based assignments require you to complete specific map objectives, like securing the extraction point on **Raven Rock**. Survival-based assignments are the hardest, requiring you to survive a certain number of waves in **Survival Mode** without anyone in your squad dying.

### Efficient Farming Strategies

For kill-based assignments, **Nightmare difficulty** is actually more efficient despite the increased risk. The zombie density is dramatically higher, meaning you'll complete kill counts faster. For objective-based assignments, focus on **Flooded**—it's the shortest Survival map and has the most linear objective path. For survival-based assignments, assemble a squad of players who understand the **co-op** dynamics and stick to the **Lighthouse** map, which offers the most defensible positions.
