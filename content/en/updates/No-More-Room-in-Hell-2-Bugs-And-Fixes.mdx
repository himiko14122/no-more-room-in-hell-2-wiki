## The Most Reported Bugs in Armageddon 1.0

The Armageddon update brought a massive wave of content—Survival Mode, the Raven Rock objective map, Solo Mode, and the Tutorial Gym—but it also introduced a fresh batch of technical issues. Understanding these bugs is the first step toward mitigating them in your sessions. The community has been vocal on forums and Discord, and the developers have responded with a series of hotfixes. Here's what's topping the charts.

### Infection Desync and Visual Glitches

One of the most frustrating **nmrih2 bugs and fixes** revolves around **NPV infection** states not syncing properly between clients. You might see a teammate at full health while their infection meter is actually at 90%. This desync can lead to sudden, unexplained deaths when the game finally catches up. The issue stems from netcode prioritization during high zombie density moments. Torn Banner has acknowledged the problem and is rolling out incremental fixes in their **nmrih2 development updates**. In the meantime, always communicate your infection status verbally and trust the **Gene Therapy** timer over visual indicators.

### Weapon Attachment and Loadout Persistence

Another common complaint involves **weapon attachments** and **loadout persistence**. Players report that custom loadouts sometimes reset to default after a match, particularly when switching between Solo Mode and co-op sessions. This bug appears linked to the new **Account XP** system, which can overwrite local configuration files. The workaround is to manually re-equip your **M7A1** or **Fierra DB12** attachments before each deployment. The developers have stated that a fix is in the works, but no definitive timeline has been announced in the **no more room in hell 2 dlc** roadmap.

### Audio Cue System Failures

The **audio cue system** is critical for survival—it tells you when a **Runner** is charging or a **Prime Zombie** is about to unleash a special attack. Unfortunately, some players experience complete audio dropout during intense firefights. This is particularly dangerous in Nightmare difficulty, where a missed cue means certain death. The bug seems to be triggered by overlapping sound channels when multiple **Walkers** and **Shamblers** are present. A temporary fix involves lowering your audio quality settings to "High" instead of "Ultra," which reduces the channel count and stabilizes the cue system.

## Practical Workarounds for Persistent Issues

While waiting for official patches, there are several workarounds you can use to minimize the impact of known bugs. These aren't permanent fixes, but they'll keep your squad alive and your progression intact.

### Managing Permadeath and Character Loss

The **permadeath** system is unforgiving, and a bug that causes unexpected character deletion is a nightmare. If you encounter a situation where your character is deleted without a clear cause, immediately exit the game and verify your save file integrity through Steam. This can sometimes restore your character. Additionally, avoid playing during server-wide events like **Weekly Assignments** if you're on a high-level character, as these events have a higher chance of triggering progression bugs.

### Optimizing Performance to Reduce Crashes

Crashes are another major category of **nmrih2 bugs and fixes**. Many crashes are related to memory leaks, particularly on the Flooded Survival map. Reducing your texture quality to "Medium" and disabling ray tracing can significantly improve stability. Also, make sure your GPU drivers are up to date, as Torn Banner has optimized the game for recent driver releases. The **crossplay** feature can also cause performance issues on older consoles, so consider disabling it if you're playing on a PS5 or Xbox Series S.
