## New Progression: Merits, Weekly Assignments, and Cosmetics

One of the most impactful changes in the **nmrih2 update** is the introduction of **Merits**, a new currency separate from **Credits**. While Credits are earned through general play and used for permanent unlocks, **Merits** are earned by completing specific **Weekly Assignments**. These are rotating challenges that refresh every seven days, encouraging players to try new loadouts and strategies. The **nmrih2 patch notes** confirm that Merits can be spent on exclusive **Cosmetics** and rare weapon skins that aren’t available through the standard progression track. This system provides a compelling reason to log in regularly and adds a layer of long-term goal setting beyond the core gameplay loop.

**Is the nmrih2 update free for existing players?**

Yes, the Armageddon 1.0 update is completely free for all existing players on PC. It was a major milestone in the game's development and was included in the base game price. The **nmrih2 armageddon update** brought all the new content and features without any additional cost.

**What is the best way to earn Merits quickly?**

Focus on the **Weekly Assignments** that align with your play style. If you're good at objective maps, prioritize those assignments. If you prefer killing, focus on weapon mastery tasks. Completing all four assignments weekly is the most efficient way to farm **Merits** for exclusive cosmetics.

**Does the nmrih2 update include any new zombie types?**

The Armageddon 1.0 update did not introduce new zombie types, but it did significantly alter the behavior of existing ones. **Runners** are more aggressive, and **Crawlers** are more resilient. The **Prime Zombie** on Raven Rock has new attack patterns that require updated strategies.

**How does the Rescue Beacon equipment work?**

The **Rescue Beacon** is a single-use item that, when activated, calls in a one-time extraction after a short delay. It can be used to save a downed teammate or to escape an overwhelming horde. It takes up a full inventory slot, so you must weigh its utility against other gear.

The initial launch had some minor desync issues between PC and console players, but the subsequent **nmrih2 hotfixes** have resolved most of these.
