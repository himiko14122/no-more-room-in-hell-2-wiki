The **no more room in hell 2 scavenger perk** increases the quantity and quality of supplies you find when looting **crates**, **lockers**, and **medical cabinets** scattered across maps like **Lighthouse** and **Government Bunker**. At base level, Scavenger grants a flat 25% bonus to loot quantity—meaning you'll pull an extra magazine or bandage from most containers. The **Expert upgrade** doubles your chance to find rare items, including **Gene Therapy** vials, **Fierra DB12** shells, and **M7A1** ammunition.

What makes Scavenger particularly valuable is its interaction with **NPV infection** management. More loot means more **Phalanx Pills** and **antiseptic** to stave off infection between safe rooms. In **Nightmare difficulty**, where supplies are scarce and **Prime Zombies** drop minimal loot, Scavenger becomes a force multiplier for your entire team.

The perk also synergizes with **Account XP** progression. ## Frequently Asked Questions

**Does the Scavenger perk affect all loot containers?**

Yes, Scavenger applies to all containers including **crates**, **lockers**, **medicine cabinets**, and **toolboxes**. It does not affect **enemy drops** or **objective rewards**. The **Expert upgrade** specifically increases rare item drops like **Gene Therapy** and **Phalanx Pills**.

**Can Scavenger stack with other players' Scavenger perks?**

No, Scavenger effects do not stack between multiple players. However, having one Scavenger per squad is sufficient—additional Scavengers waste skill points better spent on **Guts** or **Marathon Runner**. Coordinate with your team to avoid redundant perk picks.

**Is Scavenger worth it on Nightmare difficulty?**

Absolutely. **Nightmare difficulty** drastically reduces supply spawns, making Scavenger's 25% bonus more impactful than on lower difficulties. Combined with **Acquired Immunity**, Scavenger becomes essential for surviving extended runs on **Raven Rock** and **Lighthouse**.

**How does Scavenger compare to the Butcher skill?**

**Butcher** enhances melee combat damage, while Scavenger improves loot acquisition. For most builds, Scavenger provides more value because ammunition and medical supplies are universal needs. However, dedicated melee builds may prefer Butcher for raw combat potential.

**Does Scavenger require the Expert upgrade to be useful?**

No, even base Scavenger provides a meaningful 25% loot bonus. The **Expert upgrade** is recommended for **Nightmare difficulty** and **Weekly Assignments**, but casual players can benefit from Scavenger at any tier.
