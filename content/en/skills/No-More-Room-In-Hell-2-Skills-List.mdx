## How Skill Points and Unlock Levels Work

Before you can optimize your **no more room in hell 2 skills list**, you need to understand the economy behind it. However, each perk has a **skill unlock level** requirement—some are available at level 1, while others like **Caravan** and **Foreman** require you to hit level 15 or 20 before you can even slot them.

The system works like this: you earn **Account XP** from completing objectives, surviving waves, and extracting successfully. **Merits** are bonus XP awarded for specific feats, like killing a **Prime Zombie** or reviving three teammates in one match. These accumulate across all your Responders, so even if you die on a run, your progression isn't wasted.

**Butcher** is the go-to for melee-focused builds. The +20% melee damage applies to your **Sledgehammer** and any other blunt weapon, and the **Expert** upgrade adds a cleave effect that hits multiple enemies on kill. This turns you into a **Walker** blender, especially in tight corridors.

**Scavenger** is less flashy but arguably more valuable. The +15% supply drop rate from crates means more **Phalanx Pills**, more ammo, and more **Gene Therapy** kits for your team. ### Utility and Objective Perks: Caravan, Foreman, and Mule

**Caravan** speeds up objective interactions by +20%. Whether you're repairing a generator on **Lighthouse** or securing a zone on **Government Bunker**, this perk shaves critical seconds off each objective. The **Expert** upgrade adds a team-wide speed boost to objectives within 10 meters, making it a strong support pick.

**Foreman** enhances tool effectiveness, which matters for **Raven Rock's** complex hacking puzzles and **Flooded's** valve-turning sequences. The **Expert** version reduces tool durability loss by 50%, meaning your **Responder** can keep working longer without burning through supplies.

**Mule** is the quality-of-life perk for loot hoarders. +15% carry capacity (and +30% at Expert) lets you haul more **Credits**, more ammo, and more medical supplies. ## Advanced Tips for Spending Skill Points

One of the biggest mistakes new players make is spreading **skill points** too thin. In the **no more room in hell 2 skills list**, it's almost always better to max out one or two perks than to have five perks at base level. ## Frequently Asked Questions

**What are the best skills for beginners in NMRIH2?**

Start with **Marathon Runner** and **Scavenger**. Both are cheap, unlock early, and provide immediate survivability. **Marathon Runner** helps you escape dangerous situations, while **Scavenger** ensures you find enough supplies. Add **Guts** once you hit level 8 for extra damage resistance.

**How do I unlock Expert skills in the no more room in hell 2 skills list?**

Expert skills require you to reach a higher **skill unlock level** specific to that perk, then spend double the base **skill points**. For example, **Acquired Immunity Expert** requires level 30 and costs 8 skill points total. You'll earn these points by leveling up your Responder.

**Can I reset my skill points in NMRIH2?**

Currently, there is no respec system. Once you spend **skill points**, they're permanently allocated. This makes planning essential—don't waste points on perks you won't use. Focus on one or two perks and max them out before branching out.

**Does the Marathon Runner perk stack with other speed boosts?**

Yes, **Marathon Runner** stacks with temporary speed boosts from items like **Phalanx Pills**. However, the **Expert** upgrade's stamina regen effect does not stack with itself. This makes it a one-time investment that pays off throughout the match.

**What's the best perk for solo players on Nightmare difficulty?**

**Acquired Immunity** is the single best perk for solo play on Nightmare. The infection rate is brutal, and without teammates to help you, avoiding **NPV infection** is critical. Pair it with **Scavenger** for resource sustain and **Marathon Runner** for escape options.
