## How the Nmrih2 Acquired Immunity Perk Works Mechanically

The **nmrih2 acquired immunity perk** doesn't just reduce infection damage—it fundamentally changes how your **Responder** processes the **NPV infection** that plagues every map. At its base level, this perk provides a passive resistance to infection buildup, meaning you'll take longer to reach the critical infection threshold that triggers zombie conversion. This is particularly valuable on maps like **Flooded** and **Lighthouse**, where environmental hazards and **Walker** swarms can quickly overwhelm unprepared players.

The perk operates on a hidden infection meter that accumulates when you take hits from **Runners**, **Crawlers**, or **Shamblers**. Each zombie type applies a different infection value per hit, with **Prime Zombies** applying the most severe buildup. Acquired Immunity reduces this accumulation rate by a percentage that scales with your perk tier. At tier 1, you'll see roughly a 15% reduction in infection gain. At tier 2, that jumps to 30%. The **Expert upgrade** takes this further by granting complete infection immunity upon successful extraction, making it invaluable for **Nightmare runs** where even a single mistake can doom your team.

### Infection Meter Mechanics and Zombie Type Interactions

Understanding how each zombie type contributes to your infection meter is crucial for maximizing the **nmrih2 acquired immunity perk**. **Runners** apply a moderate 10-point infection value per hit, while **Crawlers** are less threatening at 5 points but often attack in groups. **Shamblers** apply 8 points but are slower, making them easier to avoid. The real threat comes from **Prime Zombies**, which apply a devastating 25-point infection value per hit. The perk's percentage reduction applies to all these values, meaning at tier 2, a **Runner** hit drops from 10 to 7 points of infection buildup.

### Environmental Infection Sources and Tier 2 Benefits

The tier 2 upgrade for the **nmrih2 acquired immunity perk** extends its protection to environmental infection sources that many players overlook. Contaminated water pools on **Flooded**, toxic gas leaks in **Government Bunker**, and even certain **Walker** corpse interactions can all contribute to infection buildup. At tier 2, these environmental sources are reduced by the same 30% reduction, making the perk significantly more valuable for map exploration. This is particularly important for **Scavenger** players who need to search off-path areas for extra supplies.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Does the nmrih2 acquired immunity perk stack with Phalanx Pills?**

Yes, the **nmrih2 acquired immunity perk** stacks multiplicatively with **Phalanx Pills**. If you have tier 2 immunity (30% reduction) and use a pill that provides 50% temporary reduction, your total infection reduction is roughly 65%. This makes the combination exceptionally powerful for surviving **Prime Zombie** encounters.

**How many skill points does the Expert upgrade require?**

The **Expert upgrade** for the **nmrih2 acquired immunity perk** costs 3 skill points total, with tier 1 costing 1 point and tier 2 costing 2 points. You'll need to reach level 20 with your **Responder** to unlock the Expert tier, so prioritize **Account XP** grinding if this is your goal.

**Is Acquired Immunity worth it for casual players?**

For casual players who primarily play **Normal** or **Hard** difficulty, the **nmrih2 acquired immunity perk** is still valuable but less critical. The infection system is less punishing at lower difficulties, so you might get more immediate value from **Scavenger** or **Butcher**. However, if you plan to attempt **Nightmare** eventually, investing early is wise.

**Can I reset my skill points after investing in Acquired Immunity?**

Currently, there's no respec system in No More Room in Hell 2. Once you invest skill points into the **nmrih2 acquired immunity perk**, they're permanently allocated. This makes it essential to plan your **nmrih2 best skill build** carefully before committing points, especially if you're working toward **Nightmare** content.

**Does the extraction immunity from Expert trigger on all maps?**

Yes, the **Expert upgrade**'s extraction immunity applies to all maps, including **Raven Rock**, **Flooded**, **Lighthouse**, and **Government Bunker**. As long as you successfully extract, any infection you've accumulated will be completely cleared, regardless of the mission type or difficulty setting.
