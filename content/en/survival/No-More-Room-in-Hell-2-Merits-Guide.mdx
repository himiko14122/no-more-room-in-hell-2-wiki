## Understanding the Merits Economy in No More Room in Hell 2

The **nmrih2 merits guide** starts with a fundamental distinction: **Credits** and **Merits** are two separate currencies with very different purposes. Credits are earned from extracting Supplies and are used for permanent unlocks like weapons and gear that persist across all your Responders. Merits, introduced in the 1.0 Armageddon update, are account-bound and earned exclusively through **Weekly Assignments**. These assignments reset every week and offer a rotating set of objectives that reward you with Merits upon completion.

What makes Merits valuable is their scarcity. Unlike Credits, which you can farm by repeatedly extracting Supplies, Merits are time-gated. Each week presents a limited pool of assignments, and once you've completed them, you're done until the next reset. This makes every Merit precious, especially when the shop rotates limited-time cosmetics and equipment that may never return.

### How Weekly Assignments Work

Weekly Assignments are divided into tiers, typically ranging from simple tasks like "Kill 50 Walkers" to more challenging objectives like "Complete a Nightmare extraction without using Phalanx Pills." Each completed assignment grants a set number of Merits, with harder tasks offering better rewards. The **nmrih2 assignments guide** recommends prioritizing assignments that align with your natural playstyle to avoid wasting time on objectives that force you into uncomfortable situations.

| Assignment Type | Typical Objective | Merit Reward | Difficulty |
|-----------------|-------------------|--------------|------------|
| Combat | Kill 100 Zombies | 15 Merits | Easy |
| Survival | Extract 3 times on any difficulty | 25 Merits | Medium |
| Challenge | Complete Nightmare extraction | 50 Merits | Hard |
| Speedrun | Extract within 15 minutes | 30 Merits | Medium |

## Difficulty Levels and Merit Farming Efficiency

The **nmrih2 difficulties explained** system directly impacts how efficiently you can farm Merits. Each difficulty tier—**Beginner**, Normal, Hard, and **Nightmare**—offers different zombie spawn rates, item rarity, and Credit multipliers. However, Merits themselves are not tied to difficulty; they're tied to assignments. This creates an interesting optimization problem: should you grind assignments on **nmrih2 beginner difficulty** for safety, or push into Nightmare for faster completion of challenge assignments?

For most players, the answer lies in a hybrid approach. Beginner difficulty is perfect for completing combat assignments quickly, as zombie density is lower and you can mow through hordes with less risk. However, challenge assignments that require Nightmare completions or specific conditions will force you into higher difficulties. The **nmrih2 nightmare guide** recommends only attempting Nightmare with a well-equipped, high-level Responder, as the risk of **permadeath** is ever-present.

### Risk vs. Reward: When to Push Higher Difficulties

| Difficulty | Zombie Count | Supply Density | Credit Multiplier | Recommended For |
|------------|--------------|----------------|-------------------|-----------------|

| Normal | Medium | Medium | 1.5x | Standard progression |
| Hard | High | Low | 2.0x | Experienced players, challenge assignments |

The key insight is that **Merits are account-bound**, so you can safely complete assignments on Beginner difficulty without risking your best Responders. Save your high-level characters for Nightmare assignments where their superior skills and gear give you a fighting chance.

## Advanced Merit Farming Strategies

To truly master the **nmrih2 merits guide**, you need to think beyond individual assignments. Here are advanced strategies that veteran players use to maximize their Merit income each week.

### Prioritize Account XP and Skills

Merits aren't the only progression currency—**Account XP** feeds into your overall account level, unlocking new skills like **Scavenger**, **Guts**, and **Acquired Immunity**. These skills provide permanent bonuses that make all future Merit farming easier. Scavenger increases supply find rates, Guts reduces infection damage, and Acquired Immunity gives you a chance to resist NPV infection entirely.

### Coordinate with Your Squad

The **nmrih2 respawn guide** emphasizes that teamwork is critical in No More Room in Hell 2. When farming Merits, coordinate with your squad to complete multiple assignments simultaneously. For example, if one assignment requires killing 100 Zombies and another requires extracting from Flooded, you can combine both objectives in a single run. This efficiency is the cornerstone of serious Merit farming.

### Track Weekly Rotation

Weekly Assignments follow a predictable rotation pattern. By tracking which assignments appear each week, you can plan your Merit farming schedule around the easiest objectives. The **nmrih2 assignments guide** recommends keeping a spreadsheet of past assignments to predict future rotations, allowing you to save your best Responders for weeks with high-value challenge assignments.

## Common Mistakes and How to Avoid Them

Even experienced players make errors when navigating the **nmrih2 merits guide** system. Here are the most common pitfalls and how to avoid them.

### Wasting Merits on Low-Value Items

The biggest mistake is spending Merits on cosmetics when you haven't secured essential survival items. Always maintain a stockpile of Phalanx Pills and at least one Gene Therapy Kit before splurging on weapon skins. Remember, **permadeath** means losing your Responder and their loadout—a Gene Therapy Kit can prevent that loss.

### Overextending on Nightmare Difficulty

The **nmrih2 nightmare guide** warns against attempting Nightmare with under-leveled Responders. The difficulty spike is severe, and the scarcity of supplies means one mistake can cascade into a team wipe. Only push Nightmare when you have maxed skills, a full squad, and a clear extraction plan.

### Ignoring the Respawn Mechanics

The **nmrih2 respawn guide** covers how respawning works in co-op: when a Responder dies, they spectate until the team reaches a respawn point. However, in Nightmare difficulty, respawns are limited or disabled entirely. Understanding these mechanics is crucial for planning Merit farming runs, especially on higher difficulties.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How many Merits can I earn per week?**

Weekly Assignments typically offer between 100-150 Merits total across all available objectives. The exact number varies based on the rotation, with challenge assignments providing the largest individual rewards. Completing all assignments every week maximizes your Merit income.

**Are Merits shared across all my Responders?**

Yes, Merits are account-bound, meaning they are shared across all your Responders. This is different from Credits, which are also account-wide. However, equipment purchased with Merits is often tied to specific Responders, so choose your purchases carefully.

**Can I farm Merits on Beginner difficulty?**

Absolutely. Combat and survival assignments can be completed on any difficulty, including Beginner. This is the safest way to earn Merits, though challenge assignments that specifically require Nightmare completions will force you into higher difficulties.

**What happens if I die with a Gene Therapy Kit in my inventory?**

If your Responder dies, their inventory is lost, including any Gene Therapy Kits. This is why it's crucial to use these items before death or stash them in your secure storage if available. Otherwise, you'll need to repurchase them with Merits.

**Do Merits reset with each season?**

No, Merits are permanent and do not reset with seasonal updates. However, the Merit shop rotates limited-time items, so if you see something you want, grab it before it disappears. Unspent Merits carry over indefinitely.
