## Permadeath, Respawn, and Team Coordination

The **Permadeath** mechanic is the most brutal aspect of **no more room in hell 2 weekly assignments**. When a **Responder** dies, they're gone permanently — their loadout, their **Skills**, their **Account XP** contribution for that run. The **respawn system** in assignments is limited to a single "reinforcement wave" that the **CRC** can trigger mid-run, but it only restores downed players, not dead ones. This creates a risk-reward calculus: do you push for the optional objective and risk losing a maxed-out **Responder**, or extract early with partial **Merits**? Veteran squads use a two-team split — one aggressive group clears objectives, one defensive group guards the extraction path. This minimizes exposure for your best players.

### Roles for Squad Coordination

Effective team composition in **no more room in hell 2 weekly assignments** requires more than just good aim. Assign a **Scout** to stay near extraction points and call out shifts, a **Medic** with **Guts** and **Field Medic** skills to manage bleed-outs and **NPV infection**, and a **Breacher** with **Scavenger** to locate **rare supplies** and **Gene Therapy** caches. The remaining players should be **Clears** focused on **Prime Zombie** elimination. This role split ensures that no single player carries the entire squad's survival burden. Rotate roles each week to keep **Account XP** gains balanced across your **Responder** roster.

Coordination extends beyond just kill assignments. You need a dedicated player tracking **NPV infection** levels across the squad, calling out when someone needs **Phalanx Pills** or a **Gene Therapy** injection. The **Gene Therapy** cure is rare in assignments, so ration it. If two players are infected, prioritize the one with the most **Account XP** — it's harsh, but the math works out. The **CRC** in-game voice prompts will also announce **Prime Zombie** spawns, but don't rely on them; assign a spotter to watch the map edges.
