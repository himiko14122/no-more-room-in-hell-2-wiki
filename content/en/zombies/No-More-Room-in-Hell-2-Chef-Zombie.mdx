## What Is the nmrih2 Chef Zombie?

The **nmrih2 chef zombie** is a special melee-oriented zombie variant in No More Room in Hell 2, classified as a heavy Walker with increased health and a devastating close-range attack. You will usually encounter it in indoor food-service areas, but it can also wander into corridors and spawn during horde events. It is one of the most dangerous **nmrih2 special zombies** because it combines the durability of a tank with the aggression of a Runner.

### Visual Identification and Lore

The chef zombie wears a blood-stained white double-breasted jacket, checkered pants, and a toque or hairnet. It carries a large chef's knife or cleaver in one hand. The lore suggests these are former kitchen staff from the restaurants around Raven Rock, infected during the early outbreak. The **CRC** (Contamination Response Corps) notes classify it as a "Food Service Anomaly" due to its tendency to remain near food storage and preparation zones. Autopsy reports confirm the presence of **NPV infection** in the brainstem, which explains its unusual resistance to pain and its relentless pursuit of survivors.

### How It Differs from Walkers, Runners, and Shamblers

The chef zombie is slower than a Runner but faster than a Shambler. It has roughly 2.5 times the health of a standard Walker. Unlike normal Walkers, the chef zombie will sometimes perform a lunging overhead swing that can knock you down. It does not sprint, but it will aggressively path toward the nearest survivor and can break through doors. This makes it a priority target in any encounter, especially when it is surrounded by weaker zombies. ## Chef Zombie Stats and Damage Profile

Understanding the **nmrih2 chef zombie** damage profile is essential if you want to survive a close encounter. Its cleaver attack deals 35 base damage on Normal difficulty, which is more than double a standard Walker's 15 damage. On **Nightmare** difficulty, that number rises to 55, and a single hit can put you into the incapacitated state if you are already wounded. The overhead slam is even more dangerous, dealing 50 damage on Normal and 70 on Nightmare, with a knockdown effect that leaves you vulnerable to the rest of the horde.

### Health and Damage Reduction

The chef zombie has 300 HP on Normal difficulty. It does not have armor, but its thick chef's coat provides a 10% damage reduction against piercing weapons like the **M7A1** rifle. Blunt weapons like the **Sledgehammer** ignore this reduction entirely, which is why melee builds are so effective against this enemy. Fire damage also bypasses the coat reduction, making molotovs a solid backup option. The **nmrih2 zombie damage** system rewards weapon variety, so always carry at least one blunt or fire option.

### Attack Patterns and Counterplay

- **Standard swipe**: 35 damage, medium range, can be blocked with a melee weapon.
- **Overhead cleaver slam**: 50 damage, knocks you down, 2-second wind-up. This is your best window to land a headshot.
- **Door break**: destroys wooden doors in 3 hits. Do not use doors as a barrier.

## Weak Spots: The nmrih2 Headshot Guide

The most reliable way to kill a **nmrih2 chef zombie** is to land consistent headshots. The head is the primary weak spot, and a headshot deals 2.5x damage. However, the chef zombie has a slightly tilted head posture, which makes it harder to hit than a normal Walker. This is why the **nmrih2 headshot guide** below focuses on positioning and timing rather than raw aim.

### Headshot Multipliers and Limb Damage

- **Headshot**: 2.5x damage
- **Neck**: 1.5x damage
- **Arms**: 0.8x damage
- **Legs**: 0.6x damage

### Limb Crippling Strategy

Shooting the legs of a chef zombie will slow it down by 40%, but it will not stop it. The arms can be crippled to reduce its attack damage by 50%. This is a viable strategy if you are using a low-damage weapon like the **Gruber 922** pistol and need to escape. Crippling the chef zombie's arms also cancels its overhead slam, giving you a much safer window to reload or reposition. These **nmrih2 zombie weak spots** are often overlooked by new players, but they can turn a deadly fight into a manageable one.

## Best Weapons and Loadouts for Chef Zombies

When you see a **nmrih2 chef zombie** approaching, your weapon choice matters more than anything else. The **Sledgehammer** is the best melee option because it ignores the chef coat's piercing resistance and can stagger the zombie on every hit. If you prefer firearms, the **Fierra DB12** shotgun is the most efficient tool at close range, dealing massive burst damage to the head.

### Firearm Recommendations

- **Fierra DB12**: 2 headshots to kill on Normal difficulty.
- **M7A1**: 8-10 headshots to kill; use burst fire and aim for the neck.
- **Gruber 922**: 14 headshots; only as a last resort.

### Melee and Throwables

The **Sledgehammer** can kill a chef zombie in 4 charged swings. Fire axes and crowbars are less effective, requiring 7-9 swings. Molotovs deal 20 damage per second and will kill a chef zombie in 15 seconds of burn time. A well-placed molotov can also stagger the chef zombie, interrupting its slam attack. If you are running a **Responder** loadout, prioritize the Fierra DB12 and Sledgehammer combination for maximum efficiency against this enemy.

## How to Deal with Chef Zombies in a Horde

The chef zombie rarely appears alone. In most cases, it is embedded inside a horde of Walkers and Runners, which makes targeting it difficult. This section covers the **nmrih2 how to deal with horde** tactics specifically for the **nmrih2 chef zombie**.

### Positioning and Kiting

Never fight a chef zombie in a narrow corridor. Its cleaver slam has a wide arc and can hit multiple survivors. Instead, pull it into an open area and kite it while your teammates focus fire on its head. Use the **Phalanx** formation: two players in front with melee weapons, two players behind with firearms. This formation keeps the chef zombie occupied while the back line deals damage. For more advanced strategies, check out our [NMRIH2 horde management](/en/zombies/nmrih2-horde-management/) guide.

### Team Roles and Communication

Assign one player as the designated chef zombie killer. That player should carry a **Fierra DB12** or **Sledgehammer**. The rest of the team should clear the surrounding Walkers and Runners. Use voice comms to call out when the chef zombie starts its overhead slam, because that is your only safe window to land a headshot. If you are playing solo, focus on kiting and use environmental hazards like fire barrels to soften the chef zombie before engaging.

## Spawn Locations, Difficulty Modes, and Progression

The **nmrih2 chef zombie** spawns most frequently in the restaurant and cafeteria sections of **Raven Rock** and the flooded kitchen areas of **Flooded**. It can also spawn during horde events on any map. On **Nightmare** and **Permadeath** difficulties, chef zombies spawn in larger numbers and can appear as early as the first objective. Knowing these spawn patterns is key to avoiding ambushes.

### Progression and Unlocks

Killing chef zombies rewards you with **Credits** and **Merits**. Credits are used to purchase weapons and supplies between runs, while Merits unlock permanent character upgrades through the **Gene Therapy** system. The **Responder** class gains bonus damage against special zombies, making it the best class for chef zombie hunting. Investing in Gene Therapy upgrades that increase headshot damage or melee stagger will make future chef zombie encounters significantly easier.

### Tips for New Players

If you are new, avoid engaging a chef zombie with the **Gruber 922**. Instead, use a **Sledgehammer** and aim for the head. Always keep a clear escape route, and do not waste your **M7A1** ammo on body shots. Remember that the chef zombie is slower than you, so you can always disengage and regroup if the fight goes badly. ## Frequently Asked Questions

**Where does the nmrih2 chef zombie spawn?**

The chef zombie spawns in restaurant, cafeteria, and kitchen areas on maps like Raven Rock and Flooded. It can also appear during horde events. On Nightmare difficulty, it can spawn in any interior zone from the first objective onward.

**What is the best weapon against the chef zombie?**

The Fierra DB12 shotgun is the best firearm, killing a chef zombie in two headshots on Normal difficulty. The Sledgehammer is the best melee weapon because it ignores the chef coat's piercing resistance and staggers the zombie on every hit.

**Does the chef zombie drop special loot?**

Yes, chef zombies drop increased Credits and Merits compared to normal Walkers. They also have a higher chance of dropping ammunition and medical supplies, making them worthwhile targets despite their danger.

**Can you outrun a chef zombie?**

Yes, the chef zombie is slower than a Runner and slightly slower than a standard Walker. You can outrun it by sprinting, but it will break through doors, so do not use a door as a barrier.

**Does the chef zombie appear in Permadeath mode?**

Yes, the chef zombie appears in Permadeath mode with the same stats as Nightmare difficulty. Because death is permanent, you should always treat a chef zombie as a priority target and never engage it without backup.
