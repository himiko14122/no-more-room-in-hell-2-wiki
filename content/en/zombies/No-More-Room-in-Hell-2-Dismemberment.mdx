## Understanding the Dismemberment System

Understanding **no more room in hell 2 dismemberment** starts with the per-limb health model. Each zombie has separate hit zones for the head, torso, left arm, right arm, left leg, and right leg. Damage applied to a specific limb reduces that limb's integrity independently. Once a limb reaches zero integrity, it is severed or crippled, permanently altering the zombie's behavior and movement patterns. This system creates dynamic combat encounters where every shot matters and positioning determines your survival.

### How Limb Damage Works

Every weapon in NMRIH2 applies damage to specific limb zones based on where your shot lands. A **Sledgehammer** swing aimed at a zombie's knee will crush the leg bone and cause the zombie to collapse into a crawling state. Bullets from the **M7A1** rifle punch through flesh but may not sever limbs in a single shot unless you land repeated hits on the same zone. Shotguns like the **Fierra DB12** excel at dismemberment because each pellet carries individual damage that can strip multiple limbs in one blast.

Limb damage also interacts with the game's **CRC** (Casualty Response Coordination) system, which tracks how many zombies you've incapacitated versus fully killed. Severing both arms of a zombie doesn't kill it, but it renders the creature harmless since it can no longer grab or attack. This is a legitimate strategy for conserving ammunition, though you'll need to finish the zombie off eventually to earn **Credits** and **Merits**.
