## What Is the No More Room in Hell 2 Police Zombie?

The police zombie is a special infected variant introduced as part of the Armageddon (1.0) update. It is visually distinct from the standard Walker: it wears the tattered remnants of a law enforcement uniform, complete with a visible stab-resistant vest and, occasionally, a riot helmet. Its most iconic feature is the pair of glowing red eyes, which is why the community often calls it the **red eyes zombie**. The red glow is not cosmetic; it is a gameplay cue that tells you this enemy has significantly more zombie health than its unarmored cousins and that torso shots will be heavily mitigated.

### Visual Identification and Lore

From a lore perspective, the police zombie represents the first responders who were overwhelmed during the initial outbreak. The **CRC** (Casualty Response Corps) and local **Responder** units were equipped to handle riots and armed suspects, not a biological catastrophe. The **NPV infection** reanimated them with their gear still intact. The vest that protected them in life now protects them in undeath. Recognizing the police zombie at a glance is critical: the red eyes are visible even in low-light environments like **Raven Rock**'s flooded tunnels, giving you a precious second to adjust your approach.

### Behavioral Profile

In terms of behavior, the police zombie moves at the same speed as a standard Walker. It does not sprint like a Runner, but it has a slightly larger aggro radius and is more resistant to stagger. A single pistol shot will not interrupt its advance, and it will keep pushing through suppressive fire. The police zombie also tends to lead hordes, acting as a damage sponge while Runners and Shamblers swarm around it. Mastering this behavior is the first step toward understanding no more room in hell 2 **horde mechanics**.

If you shoot the body, you are effectively dealing only 40% of your weapon's damage. Limbs take full damage, but limb shots do not benefit from the headshot multiplier. The head is completely unarmored and takes 3.0x damage, making the skull the only viable target for low-damage weapons. ## Frequently Asked Questions

**How much health does a police zombie have in No More Room in Hell 2?**

A police zombie has 120 base health, more than double a standard Walker's 50 HP. On Nightmare difficulty, this value scales upward, and the vest's 60% torso damage reduction effectively doubles its survivability against body shots. Bring armor-piercing rounds or aim exclusively for the head.

**What is the best way to kill a police zombie?**

The best way is a single Fierra DB12 headshot at close range. If you lack a shotgun, use a Sledgehammer to the head twice. Always aim for the head, as the vest provides 60% damage reduction to torso shots.

**Are police zombies the same as the red eyes zombie?**

Yes. The community calls the police zombie the red eyes zombie because of its distinctive glowing red eyes, which signal its armored status and high health pool. Both terms refer to the same enemy type in patch notes and community guides.

**Can Runners be police zombies?**

No, police zombies are always Walker-speed variants and never sprint. However, Runners frequently spawn alongside police zombies in mixed hordes, so you must prioritize killing the Runners first before engaging the armored target. A Runner that reaches your line can stagger you and break the Phalanx.

**Do police zombies spawn more on Nightmare?**

Yes. Nightmare difficulty increases the police zombie spawn rate to roughly 20% of all zombies, and they can appear in groups of three or more. This makes armor-piercing tactics and melee specialists essential for survival, especially when Permadeath is enabled and a single mistake can end your run.
