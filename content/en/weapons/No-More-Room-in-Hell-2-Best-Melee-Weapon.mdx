## Melee Combat Fundamentals in NMRIH2

Before we crown the **nmrih2 best melee weapon**, you need to understand how melee combat actually works in NMRIH2. Shamblers are the slow, shambling corpses that wander aimlessly. They're easy to dispatch with any melee weapon, but they often appear in groups. Walkers are faster and more aggressive, capable of closing distance quickly if you're not paying attention. Runners are the true threat: they sprint at you and can cover 20 meters in seconds. Your **stamina bar** is your most precious resource in melee combat.
