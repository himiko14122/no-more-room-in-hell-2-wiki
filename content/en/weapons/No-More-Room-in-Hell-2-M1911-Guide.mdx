## M1911 Overview and Core Stats

The M1911 in No More Room in Hell 2 is a semi-automatic pistol chambered in .45 ACP. It is a **Responder**-class weapon available early in your progression, and it serves as a reliable backup for any primary weapon. Unlike the **nmrih2 m9a3 pistol**, which holds 17 rounds of 9mm, the M1911 holds only 7 rounds in its standard magazine. What it lacks in capacity, it makes up for in damage per shot, and that trade-off defines the entire M1911 experience.

| Stat | M1911 Value |
| --- | --- |
| Caliber | .45 ACP |
| Magazine Capacity | 7 rounds (8 with extended mag) |
| Damage per Shot | High (one-shot headshot on standard Walkers) |
| Fire Rate | Semi-automatic, moderate |
| Reload Speed | Slow (empty reload penalty) |
| Effective Range | Medium |
| Recoil | High |
| Noise Level | Loud (suppressor recommended) |

The M1911's damage profile makes it one of the best sidearms for dealing with **Walkers**. A single headshot at close to medium range will drop a standard Walker, and even body shots can stagger enemies long enough for you to create distance. Against **Runners**, the M1911's high damage can stop them in their tracks with one or two shots, though the slow fire rate means you need to land those shots under pressure. **Shamblers**, the slow but tanky variant, require multiple headshots, so you will want to conserve ammo or switch to a primary weapon when they appear.

One of the most important things to understand about the M1911 is its reload behavior. If you empty the magazine completely, the slide locks back and the reload animation takes noticeably longer. This **nmrih2 m1911 guide** strongly recommends reloading before the magazine is empty whenever you have a safe moment. Tactical reloads keep you in the fight and prevent the dreaded empty-reload penalty that has killed many players on **Nightmare** difficulty.

## Best Attachments and Loadouts

### Optics and Muzzle Attachments

The M1911 can equip a variety of attachments. For optics, the iron sights are serviceable, but a red dot sight improves target acquisition, especially on maps like **Raven Rock** where long sightlines are common. For the muzzle, a suppressor is highly recommended. The M1911 is already loud, and in No More Room in Hell 2, noise attracts the horde. A suppressor reduces your audible footprint significantly and allows you to clear rooms without alerting every enemy on the floor. This **nmrih2 m1911 guide** suggests prioritizing the suppressor above all other attachments.

### Magazine and Grip Choices

The extended magazine attachment increases capacity from 7 to 8 rounds. It does not sound like much, but that extra round can save your life during a reload. A laser sight improves hip-fire accuracy, which is useful when you are being swarmed and do not have time to aim down sights. For players who prefer a lightweight build, the M1911 with no attachments is still a solid choice, but the suppressor alone is worth the inventory slot.
