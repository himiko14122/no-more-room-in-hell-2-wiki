## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is the M7A1 worth using in No More Room in Hell 2?**

Yes. The no more room in hell 2 m7a1 rifle is one of the most balanced primaries in the game. It handles well at mid-range, accepts strong attachments, and synergizes with both Phalanx and CRC perk trees. It's a top-tier choice for objective modes.

**What attachments work best on the M7A1?**

The best setup is a Red Dot Sight, Vertical Grip, and Extended Mag. The suppressor is a good alternative if you're playing solo or on Flooded. Avoid the 4x scope because it slows your ADS speed too much for close-quarters fights.

**How does the M7A1 compare to the Fierra DB12?**

The Fierra DB12 deals massive burst damage at close range but requires constant reloads. The M7A1 is more versatile and better at range. If you're defending a chokepoint, the DB12 wins; if you need an all-rounder, the M7A1 is better.

**Where can I find the M7A1 in Raven Rock?**

The M7A1 spawns in military supply crates in the underground bunker area of Raven Rock. It also appears in later objective stages on most maps, including Flooded. On Nightmare difficulty, spawns are much rarer, so loot with your team and call out finds in voice chat.

**Does the M7A1 work in Nightmare mode?**

Yes, but you need to manage ammo carefully. Bring a melee weapon like the Sledgehammer or fire axe as backup, and invest in the CRC Ammo Scrounger skill to increase your reserve ammo by 20%. The M7A1 remains one of the best primaries even on the hardest difficulty.
