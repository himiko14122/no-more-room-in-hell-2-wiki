## Common Mistakes and Progression Pitfalls

### Avoiding the Kill-Grind Trap

The most common mistake in the **no more room in hell 2 unlock cosmetics** grind is ignoring **Weekly Assignments** in favor of grinding kills. Kills give you **Responder XP**, but they give you almost no **Account XP**. If your goal is to unlock loadout slots and cosmetic items, you need to focus on objectives and assignments. A single supply extraction grants roughly double the **Account XP** of a full wave of kills, so always prioritize the mission objective over the body count. This is especially important in the early game when you are trying to hit account level 5 for your third loadout slot.

### Understanding Permadeath Risks to Your Cosmetics

**Permadeath** is the biggest trap for new players. When your **Responder** dies on **Nightmare** difficulty, that character is gone permanently, along with all their unlocked customization slots. Your **Account XP** and **Merits** are safe, but you lose the character-specific progress. This means you should never take a **Responder** with valuable cosmetics into a **Nightmare** run unless you are prepared to lose them. Many experienced players keep a separate "expendable" **Responder** for high-risk runs, reserving their main character with premium cosmetics for safer scenarios. This strategy preserves your investment while still allowing you to tackle the hardest content.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the fastest way to earn Account XP for cosmetics?

Focus on completing objectives and extracting supplies rather than killing zombies. ### Do cosmetics transfer between Responders?

No, most cosmetic items are tied to the specific **Responder** you purchased them for. However, **Merits**-purchased items can be re-applied to any **Responder** you own, though you need to unlock the customization slots on each character first. **Responder backgrounds** are per-character, so you need to level up each **Responder** to unlock their background slots.

### What happens to cosmetics when a Responder dies on Permadeath?

When a **Responder** dies on **Nightmare** difficulty, all character-specific unlocks are lost, including outfit pieces, voice packs, and backgrounds. Your **Account XP**, **Credits**, and **Merits** are preserved, so you can re-purchase items for a new **Responder**. This is why many players keep a separate "expendable" **Responder** for high-risk runs.

### How many loadout slots can I unlock in total?

You can unlock a maximum of five loadout slots. You start with two, unlock the third at account level 5, the fourth at level 10, and the fifth at level 20. Each loadout slot can have its own cosmetic configuration, so five slots give you substantial flexibility for different scenarios and playstyles.
