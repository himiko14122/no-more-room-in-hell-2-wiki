## Understanding the Responder Customization System

The **Responder** is your persistent character in NMRIH2, and customization operates on two distinct tracks: **appearance** and **equipment visuals**. Appearance covers your **face**, **hair**, **body type**, and the outfit you wear into battle. Equipment visuals, meanwhile, affect how your **M7A1** or **Fierra DB12** looks in your hands. Both tracks are gated by your **responder progression**, which is separate from your **account XP**. Your account-level progression unlocks **loadout slots** and advanced Campsite features, while individual Responder XP is earned by surviving scenarios and completing objectives.

### How to Change Outfit in NMRIH2

Changing your outfit happens exclusively at the **Campsite** hub before queuing for a match. Navigate to the Responder tab, select your active character, and choose "Customize." Here you'll find all unlocked clothing items, which are categorized into **headgear**, **torso**, **legs**, and **footwear**. Each piece is purchased with **Credits**—the primary currency earned from match completions—while rarer items require **Merits**, which drop from **Weekly Assignments** and high-difficulty extractions. The **nmrih2 how to change outfit** process is straightforward, but the real depth lies in mix-and-matching pieces to create a distinct silhouette that teammates can recognize at a glance during intense firefights.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can you change your Responder's appearance after creation?**

Yes, you can modify your Responder's appearance at the **Campsite** hub at any time. Navigate to the Responder tab, select "Customize," and you'll be able to adjust **face**, **hair**, **body type**, and **outfit** pieces. Changes are saved instantly and cost nothing—only unlocking new items requires **Credits** or **Merits**.

**Do no more room in hell 2 cosmetics affect gameplay?**

No, all cosmetics are purely visual. However, **voice lines** can indirectly affect gameplay by helping teammates distinguish your callouts during chaotic fights. The **compass cosmetic** is also purely visual, though some players report it improves their situational awareness due to the distinct color scheme.

**How do I get Merits quickly?**

The fastest way to earn **Merits** is by completing **Weekly Assignments**, which reset every Tuesday. Focus on **Nightmare difficulty** runs and extraction-heavy objectives, as these award the highest Merit payouts. Participating in limited-time events also grants bonus **Merits**.

**Is there a limit to how many Responders I can create?**

Yes, the number of **Responders** you can create is capped by your **account level**. Early on, you'll have access to two Responder slots, and additional slots unlock as you progress. Check your account progression screen at the **Campsite** to see your current cap.

**Do cosmetics transfer between platforms?**

Yes, thanks to **cross-progression**, all unlocked cosmetics, **Credits**, and **Merits** carry over across platforms. Your **Responder** data is tied to your account, so you can switch between PC and console without losing your cosmetic progress.
