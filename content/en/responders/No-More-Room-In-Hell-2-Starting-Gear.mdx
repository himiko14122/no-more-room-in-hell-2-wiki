**How do I unlock more loadout slots in NMRIH2?**

Loadout slots unlock through account level progression, not responder rank. Earn account XP by completing objectives, surviving scenarios, extracting supplies, and finishing weekly Assignments. Each new account level grants additional slots—the throwable slot typically unlocks around level 5 and the equipment slot (rescue beacon) around level 10. **Does responder rank affect my starting gear?**

No, responder rank does not affect your starting gear or combat capabilities. The no more room in hell 2 responder rank system only unlocks customization options like appearance slots, outfits, and voice lines. Your account level determines loadout slots and gear availability, so a level 1 responder on a high-level account can carry the same equipment as a max-level responder.

**What is the best responder for solo play in NMRIH2?**

Since all responders share identical base stats, the best responder for solo play depends entirely on your loadout choices. The Scout loadout with a suppressed sidearm and crowbar performs best for solo beginners because it emphasizes stealth and resource conservation. The rescue beacon equipment slot, unlocked at account level 10, is highly recommended for solo runs as a safety net.

**How does the Campsite help with starting gear preparation?**

The Campsite is your pre-match hub for editing loadouts, customizing responders, and checking weekly Assignments to plan XP gains.
