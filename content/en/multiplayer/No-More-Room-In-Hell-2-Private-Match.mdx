## Network Settings and Lag Fixes for Private Lobbies

Even with a private lobby, network problems can ruin the experience. The most common issue is **lag**, which manifests as rubber-banding zombies or delayed hit registration. Here are the **no more room in hell 2 network settings** that actually matter:

- **Region Locking**: Always set your server region to match your squad's physical location. Cross-region play (e.g., US host with EU players) introduces 100-200ms latency that makes headshots on **Runners** nearly impossible.

- **Bandwidth Cap**: Set this to **Unlimited** if your internet upload speed exceeds 10 Mbps. The default cap can throttle the host's ability to stream zombie AI data to 7 other players.

- **Packet Rate**: Increase from 30 to 60 if you have a stable connection. This doubles the frequency of position updates, reducing interpolation errors during **Shambler** hordes.

### FPS Boost for Hosts

Hosting a private match puts extra strain on your CPU because you're processing all zombie AI. The **fps boost** techniques that work in single-player also apply here, but with more urgency. Even with perfect **network settings**, problems can arise. Here are the most frequent issues and their solutions:
