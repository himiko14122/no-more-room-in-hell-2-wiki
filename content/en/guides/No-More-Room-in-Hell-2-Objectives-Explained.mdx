Extraction unlocks once your team secures 50% of the objective's total supply value, so a partial haul is a legitimate win condition. When the objective marker updates and the extraction zone is identified, commit immediately instead of chasing the remaining 50% supply value.

A first-run loadout that respects the weight ceiling pairs one sidearm (M1911 or M9A3), one melee weapon (Crowbar or Fire Axe), and exactly one throwable (Satchel Charge or Molotov). ## Looting Mechanics and Supply Management


### Supply Crate Resupply Inside Objectives
Supply crates spawn inside objective zones rather than in the open map, which forces resupply runs to happen under active threat. Treat each crate as a checkpoint: call out its location over proximity voice chat and re-form on the team flank before anyone dips back to restock. Never split the group across multiple floors during early objectives — a lone player looting a crate is the easiest Runner pick-off in the game. Since overencumbered players run slower, swing slower, and cannot climb, dump non-essential weight before the crate run, not after.

### Phalanx Pills vs Gene Therapy: Delay vs Cure
Phalanx pills delay NVR infection but never cure it — the infection will eventually kill your character for the run unless a cure is found. Gene Therapy appears only once per match, cures the infection, and grants immunity for the rest of the match, so it belongs to the operator you intend to extract. Wasting Gene Therapy on a low-level responder is the single biggest mistake new players make. The correct sequence: pop Phalanx pills to buy time while pushing toward the extraction zone, then trigger Gene Therapy only when extraction is imminent.
