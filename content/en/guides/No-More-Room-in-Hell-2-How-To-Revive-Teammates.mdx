## The Downed State: What Happens When a Responder Drops

When a teammate's health reaches zero, they do not die immediately. Instead, they enter the downed state, crawling on the ground with a limited bleed-out timer. This is where nmrih2 how to revive teammates becomes critical. The downed **Responder** can still see their surroundings, call out enemy positions, and even fire a **Gruber 922** sidearm if they have one equipped, but they cannot move or heal themselves.

The bleed-out timer varies by difficulty. On standard missions, you have roughly 60 seconds. On **Nightmare**, that window shrinks to 30 seconds, and the horde AI becomes more aggressive about targeting downed players. A downed **Responder** also attracts more zombie attention, so the revive window is a tactical decision, not just a reflex. Experienced squads treat the downed state as an opportunity to reposition, not a panic moment.

### Revive Syringe: The Core Tool

The **Revive Syringe** is the primary tool for bringing a teammate back. It can be found in medical crates, looted from supply caches, or purchased with **Credits** at the loadout screen. Each syringe takes one inventory slot and weighs 0.5 units, so it fits into most **nmrih2 loot guide** recommendations for medical essentials.

Using a syringe is not instant. The reviving player must hold the interact button for about 4 seconds while crouched next to the downed teammate. During this time, you are completely vulnerable. If you take damage from a **Walker** or a **Runner**, the revive progress resets. This makes positioning and area control essential. For a deeper look at syringe spawns and usage, check the [nmrih2 revive syringe guide](/en/guides/nmrih2-revive-syringe/).

### The Bleed-Out Timer and Health Recovery

When a teammate is revived, they return with 40% health and a brief invulnerability window of 2 seconds. That window is enough to stand up and equip a weapon, but not enough to escape a **Shambler** grab. After revival, the teammate's **NPV infection** level stays exactly where it was before they dropped, so a heavily infected **Responder** can still turn into a zombie after being revived.

The revived player also suffers a temporary stamina penalty for 10 seconds, making it harder to sprint away from a horde. This is why the reviving player should call out a retreat path before starting the revive. Many squads use **Phalanx pills** after a revive to restore stamina and reduce infection buildup, which we cover in the loadout section below.

## Step-by-Step: How to Revive a Teammate Safely

Now that you understand the downed state, here is the exact sequence for nmrih2 how to revive teammates in a live mission. This process works on all maps, including **Flooded** and Raven Rock, and applies to both solo play with AI companions and full co-op squads.

| Step | Action | Time | Risk Level |
|------|--------|------|------------|
| 1 | Call out the downed teammate's position | 1 sec | Low |
| 2 | Clear all nearby zombies with melee or firearms | 5-10 sec | Medium |
| 3 | Crouch next to the downed teammate | 1 sec | Low |
| 4 | Hold interact with the Revive Syringe equipped | 4 sec | High |
| 5 | Cover the revived teammate during their stand-up | 2 sec | Medium |
| 6 | Retreat to a safe position or barricade | 5 sec | Medium |

### Clearing the Area First

The single biggest mistake in nmrih2 how to revive teammates is starting the revive before clearing the area. A single **Walker** can interrupt the progress, and a **Runner** can down the reviving player too, creating a double-down situation. Use a **Sledgehammer** for quiet crowd control or a **Fierra DB12** shotgun for emergency room clearing, but remember that gunfire attracts more zombies.

If you have a teammate covering you, assign one player to watch the flanks while the other revives. The covering player should prioritize **Runners** first, since they close distance fastest, then **Walkers**, and finally **Shamblers**, which are slow but dangerous if they reach the downed player.

### Using Barricades to Secure a Revive

This is where **nmrih2 barricade guide** knowledge becomes essential. Before starting a revive, check if there is a door or window nearby that can be barricaded. A single wooden plank placed on a door buys you 10-15 seconds of safety, which is often enough to complete the revive and retreat.

Barricades are not permanent. Zombies will break through them, and the noise attracts more enemies. Use barricades as a temporary shield, not a long-term solution. If you are in an open area with no barricade options, consider dragging the downed teammate to cover. In the current build, you can grab a downed teammate and pull them up to 5 meters, which is enough to get them behind a car or into a doorway.

## Revive Mechanics and Risk Management

The revive system in NMRIH2 is designed to punish reckless play. Understanding the risk-reward balance is what separates good squads from great ones. Let us break down the key mechanics that affect nmrih2 how to revive teammates.

### Gene Therapy and Permanent Death

If a downed teammate's bleed-out timer expires, they die permanently for that mission. **Gene Therapy** is the only way to bring them back, and it consumes a valuable resource shared across your entire account. **Gene Therapy** can be purchased with **Merits** or found as a rare drop in supply crates.

Using **Gene Therapy** mid-mission is a strategic decision. It takes 8 seconds to apply, longer than a standard revive, and it leaves the revived player with only 25% health. Most squads save **Gene Therapy** for **Nightmare** runs or for teammates with high-level gear that would be lost otherwise. The **nmrih2 mission guide** on the official forums recommends reserving **Gene Therapy** for the final objective, not early skirmishes.

### Infection Levels and Revive Priority

A teammate's **NPV infection** level affects revive priority. If a teammate is above 70% infection, reviving them is often a waste of resources, since they will turn into a zombie within minutes anyway. Instead, focus on teammates with low infection who can still contribute to the mission.

**Phalanx pills** reduce infection by 15% per dose and restore 20 stamina. They are the best post-revive consumable in the game. However, they are rare, so prioritize them for players who are about to hit the 70% infection threshold. The **nmrih2 loot guide** recommends carrying at least one **Phalanx pill** per player in your squad, plus one spare for emergency revives.

## Advanced Revive Tactics for Nightmare Difficulty

**Nightmare** mode changes the revive game entirely. The bleed-out timer drops to 30 seconds, zombie spawn rates increase by 40%, and **Runners** spawn in packs of three or more. Here is how to adapt your nmrih2 how to revive teammates strategy for the hardest difficulty.

### The Two-Player Pull Method

On **Nightmare**, the 5-meter drag is your best friend. One player grabs the downed teammate and pulls them toward a safe room while the other player covers the retreat with an **M7A1** rifle or **Fierra DB12** shotgun. This method is slower than a direct revive, but it drastically reduces the risk of interruption.

Once you reach a safe room, barricade the door and then start the revive. The **nmrih2 barricade guide** for **Nightmare** recommends placing two planks on the door and one on any window before starting the revive. This gives you a full 20-30 seconds of safety, which is enough for the 4-second revive plus a 10-second recovery window.

### Kiting the Horde Away

If you cannot reach a safe room, the covering player should kite the horde away from the downed teammate. This means sprinting in a wide circle, managing stamina carefully, and leading zombies away from the revive spot. The kiter should use a **Gruber 922** pistol for self-defense, since it is lightweight and does not slow you down.

The downed teammate can help by firing their sidearm at approaching zombies, but this attracts more enemies. On **Nightmare**, it is often better for the downed player to stay silent and let the kiter do the work. Communication is key, so use voice chat or the ping system to coordinate. If you are new to the game, the [nmrih2 tutorial](/en/guides/nmrih2-tutorial/) covers the basics of movement and communication.

### When to Abandon a Revive

Sometimes, the smartest move is to leave a teammate behind. Knowing nmrih2 how to revive teammates also means knowing when not to revive at all. If the downed player is above 70% infection, if the horde is too large, or if the mission objective is about to fail, abandoning the revive is the correct call. This is a hard lesson for new players, but veteran squads know that mission success matters more than individual survival.

If you abandon a revive, make sure to loot the downed player's inventory. Their **M7A1**, ammunition, and medical supplies should not go to waste. This is where **nmrih2 how to carry more** knowledge becomes useful, since you will need to redistribute gear across the remaining squad members.
