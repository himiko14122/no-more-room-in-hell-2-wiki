The **nmrih2 government bunker** is the centerpiece of the Raven Rock scenario, a sprawling military installation that demands more than raw firepower. Unlike the open streets of Lewiston or the tight corridors of Beaulieu Hospital, this map tests your squad's ability to manage **NPV infection**, coordinate under **Nightmare difficulty**, and navigate a labyrinth of blast doors, server rooms, and decontamination chambers. This guide breaks down every section of the bunker, from the initial perimeter breach to the final helicopter extraction, with specific loadout suggestions, **skill synergies**, and the **meta picks** that separate successful runs from wipes.

## Understanding the Raven Rock Scenario Structure

The **nmrih2 government bunker** isn't a single linear path—it's a multi-phase operation that unfolds across distinct zones. Each zone introduces new threats and objectives that require different tactical approaches. The scenario begins outside the perimeter fence, where stealth is paramount, and escalates into intense interior fights where **Walker** and **Runner** hordes converge from multiple angles.

### Phase 1: Perimeter Breach and Stealth Entry

Your squad spawns at the tree line overlooking the bunker's outer fence. The first objective is to locate a maintenance hatch that bypasses the main gate. This phase rewards **Scavenger** skill users who can spot the subtle path markers and avoid unnecessary firefights. The perimeter patrols consist of scattered **Shamblers** and a few **Crawlers** near the fence line—manageable, but any gunfire alerts the interior defenders and triggers a **Prime Zombie** spawn at the main entrance.

Key positioning: assign one **Responder** to scout ahead while the rest of the squad holds a defensive line at the tree cover. Use **suppressed pistols** like the Gruber 922 to eliminate lone walkers without raising the alarm. The **Sledgehammer** is also viable here for silent melee takedowns, though it leaves you vulnerable if a **Runner** spots you from range.

### Phase 2: Decontamination Chamber and Keycard Retrieval

Once inside, the bunker's first major choke point is the decontamination chamber. This narrow corridor forces your squad into a single-file formation, which can be deadly if a horde spawns behind you. The objective here is to retrieve a **keycard** from a locked supply room adjacent to the chamber. The room contains valuable **Gene Therapy** injectors and **Phalanx Pills**, but accessing it requires solving a simple circuit puzzle while holding off incoming zombies.

**Table: Phase 2 Keycard Room Loot Table**

| Item | Rarity | Use Case |
|------|--------|----------|
| Gene Therapy | Common | Reduces NPV infection by 15% |
| Phalanx Pills | Uncommon | Temporary health boost + damage resist |
| Fierra DB12 | Rare | High-damage shotgun for close quarters |
| M7A1 Ammo | Common | Restocks primary rifle magazines |

The decontamination chamber also features a **security console** that can be hacked to seal off the eastern corridor. This is a **meta pick** for squads running **Nightmare difficulty**, as it creates a temporary safe zone for reviving downed teammates. However, hacking takes 10 seconds and leaves you exposed—coordinate with a **Guts** skill user to tank damage while the hack completes.

## Interior Defenses and Horde Management

The bunker's interior is a maze of identical-looking corridors, making navigation a challenge even for experienced players. The **nmrih2 government bunker** uses a modular layout that randomizes certain room connections each run, so memorizing a single route won't work. Instead, focus on **objective markers** and environmental cues like color-coded door frames—blue for supply rooms, red for locked security zones, yellow for extraction-related areas.

### Mess Hall Horde Fight and Ammo Conservation

The mess hall is the first major combat arena inside the bunker. Tables and overturned chairs provide partial cover, but the open layout means **Runners** can close distance quickly. The trigger for this horde fight is picking up the **mess hall keycard**, which spawns three waves of enemies: a **Walker** swarm from the north entrance, a mixed group of **Crawlers** and **Shamblers** from the kitchen, and a final wave featuring a **Prime Zombie** supported by **Runners**.

Ammo conservation is critical here. The **M7A1** is the workhorse rifle, but its magazine drains fast when you're panic-firing. Instead, prioritize **headshots** with the **Fierra DB12** at close range and switch to melee finishers for staggered enemies. The **Sledgehammer** one-shots standard walkers and can stagger **Prime Zombies** with a charged swing, making it invaluable for conserving rifle ammunition.

### Server Room Puzzle and Gene Therapy Stash

After the mess hall, the next objective is the server room, where you must activate three terminals in a specific order to unlock the **armory door**. The correct sequence is randomized each run, but the terminals emit a distinct hum when you're near the right one. Use audio cues and assign one player to call out the sequence to avoid wasting time on wrong picks.

The server room also contains a hidden **Gene Therapy stash** behind a breakable wall panel. This stash provides enough injectors to cure two squad members of **NPV infection**, which is crucial for long runs. The **Acquired Immunity** skill reduces the effectiveness of these injectors but provides passive resistance—weigh the tradeoff based on your squad's composition.

## Extraction Phase and Helicopter Defense

The final phase of the **nmrih2 government bunker** is the extraction sequence. After securing the armory and retrieving the **classified documents**, you must make your way to the surface helipad and defend the extraction point for 90 seconds while the helicopter lands. This is the most intense part of the scenario, with continuous horde spawns and limited cover.

### Helipad Defensive Positions

The helipad is exposed on three sides, making it vulnerable to **Runner** rushes from the perimeter fence. The optimal defensive setup is a triangle formation: two players holding the north and south stairwells, one player covering the eastern catwalk, and one player as a **roamer** who responds to breaches. The **Guts** skill user should anchor the center, drawing aggro while others focus on **headshot** kills.

**Table: Helipad Defense Roles**

| Role | Position | Priority Target | Recommended Weapon |
|------|----------|-----------------|-------------------|
| North Guard | Stairwell | Runners | Fierra DB12 |
| South Guard | Ramp | Walkers | M7A1 |
| Catwalk Cover | Elevated | Shamblers | Gruber 922 |
| Roamer | Center | Prime Zombies | Sledgehammer |

During the extraction, a **Prime Zombie** spawns at the 60-second mark. This tanky enemy has a weak point on its back—coordinate a flanking maneuver to expose it. The **Sledgehammer** charged attack can stagger it, creating an opening for the rest of the squad to focus fire. Once the helicopter lands, **permadeath** becomes a real threat: any player downed in the final 10 seconds will not be revived, so prioritize positioning over aggressive pushes.

## Loadout Optimization and Skill Synergies

Your loadout for the **nmrih2 government bunker** should prioritize versatility over raw power. The map's mix of tight corridors and open arenas means you'll need both close-range and mid-range options. The **Fierra DB12** is the **meta pick** for primary weapons due to its one-shot kill potential on standard zombies, but it struggles at range—pair it with a **Gruber 922** as a sidearm for emergency shots.

### Best Skill Combinations for Bunker Runs

**Scavenger** is arguably the strongest skill for this map, as it increases the chance of finding rare loot in supply rooms and increases ammo drops from zombies. Combine it with **Guts** for a frontline tank build that can survive prolonged horde fights. **Acquired Immunity** is a situational pick—it reduces the effectiveness of **Gene Therapy** but provides passive **NPV infection** resistance, which is valuable on longer runs where injectors run scarce.

The **nmrih2 government bunker** has fewer supply caches than other maps, so every ammo box and **Phalanx Pill** matters. Additionally, the **Weekly Assignments** often feature bunker-specific objectives like "extract with 0 deaths" or "complete without using Gene Therapy"—plan your skill loadout accordingly.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How long does a typical nmrih2 government bunker run take?**

A standard run lasts 25-35 minutes on normal difficulty. **Nightmare difficulty** runs can stretch to 45+ minutes due to increased zombie density and reduced ammo drops. The extraction phase alone takes 90 seconds of sustained defense, so plan your consumables accordingly.

**What is the best weapon for the bunker's tight corridors?**

The **Fierra DB12** shotgun is the **meta pick** for close-quarters combat. Its one-shot kill potential on standard zombies and ability to stagger **Prime Zombies** make it invaluable in the decontamination chamber and server room. Pair it with a **Gruber 922** for emergency long-range shots.

**Can you skip the mess hall horde fight?**

No, the mess hall keycard is mandatory to progress. However, you can reduce the horde size by hacking the **security console** in the decontamination chamber beforehand. This seals the eastern corridor, forcing fewer zombies to spawn during the fight.

**How do you cure NPV infection in the bunker?**

**Gene Therapy** injectors are the primary cure, found in supply rooms and the hidden stash in the server room. **Phalanx Pills** temporarily suppress symptoms but don't cure the infection. The **Acquired Immunity** skill provides passive resistance, reducing the need for injectors.

**What rewards do you get for completing the bunker on Nightmare?**

Completing the **nmrih2 government bunker** on **Nightmare difficulty** grants bonus **merits**, exclusive **cosmetics**, and a significant **Account XP** boost. You also unlock the "Bunker Veteran" title for your **Responder** profile, which displays in lobbies.
