## Beaulieu Hospital: The Starter Gauntlet

**No more room in hell 2 all maps** guides typically start with Beaulieu Hospital because it is the most forgiving environment for new players. The map is a multi-story medical facility with wide hallways, multiple stairwells, and a rooftop extraction zone. The **CRC** (Casualty Response Corps) has set up triage stations throughout the building, and your squad must activate three **Responder** terminals before the extraction helicopter arrives. The hospital is also the best map for learning the **NPV infection** mechanics, as the infection meter rises slowly here compared to the **Nightmare** difficulty maps.

### Hospital Layout and Critical Objectives

The hospital is divided into three wings: the Emergency Wing, the Surgical Wing, and the Administrative Wing. Each wing contains a terminal that must be activated within a time limit. The **NPV infection** spreads fastest in the Emergency Wing, where dense **Shambler** clusters block the main corridor. Use the stairwells to bypass the ground floor chaos, but beware of **Runner** ambushes on the second-floor landing. The rooftop extraction is the final objective, and defending it requires a **Phalanx** heavy gunner to hold the stairwell choke point while the rest of the team covers the helipad.

### Beaulieu Hospital Weapon Drops

Beaulieu Hospital has the most generous weapon spawn table in the game. You can find the **Fierra DB12** double-barrel shotgun in the surgical supply room, the **Gruber 922** pistol in the nurse's station, and the **M7A1** rifle in the security office. The **Sledgehammer** spawns in the maintenance closet near the boiler room. For early wins, prioritize grabbing the **Fierra DB12** and conserving **Credits** for medical supplies rather than ammunition. The **CRC** supply crates scattered through the wings also contain free bandages and antivirals, so check every corner before moving to the next objective.

## Government Bunker: The Claustrophobic Grind

The **no more room in hell 2 bunker map** is a stark contrast to the hospital. The Government Bunker is a subterranean facility with narrow corridors, reinforced doors, and a central command center. The objective is to restore power to three generators while defending against waves of **Walkers** that funnel through the main entrance. The map's claustrophobic design makes **Sledgehammer** melee combat surprisingly viable, especially in the generator rooms where firing a shotgun can attract unwanted attention from the **Runner** packs patrolling the ventilation shafts.

### Bunker Defense Phases

The bunker defense is divided into three phases. Phase one requires clearing the dormitory wing and activating the first generator. Phase two triggers a massive **Runner** swarm in the mess hall, and phase three is a final stand in the command center. Each phase grants **Merits** that can be spent on **Gene Therapy** upgrades, which permanently improve your character's health, stamina, or infection resistance. The bunker is also one of the best maps for farming **Credits** because the generator objectives have generous time bonuses. Veteran squads often run this map repeatedly to stockpile **Merits** before attempting **Raven Rock** or the **Night of the Living Dead** scenario.

## Raven Rock: The Mountain Fortress

**No more room in hell 2 raven rock** is the most complex map in the rotation, featuring a multi-level mountain facility with outdoor cliff paths, an underground research lab, and a summit extraction point. The map's verticality makes it a nightmare for players who rely on flat-line-of-sight shooting. The **NPV infection** here has mutated, producing faster **Runners** that scale the cliff faces and drop onto unsuspecting players. Veterans of **no more room in hell 2 all maps** agree that Raven Rock is where the real challenge begins, as the **Permadeath** mode on this map punishes even the smallest positioning mistake.

### Raven Rock Secret Areas and Shortcuts

Raven Rock is packed with hidden rooms and shortcuts that veteran players use to shave minutes off their objective times. The research lab contains a secret armory behind a breakable wall, stocked with the **M7A1** and a full set of tactical gear. The maintenance shaft near the lower entrance is a shortcut to the summit that bypasses the main horde, but it requires a **Sledgehammer** to break the rusted grate. For a full breakdown of these hidden locations, check out our [Raven Rock secrets guide](/en/maps/no-more-room-in-hell-2-raven-rock-secrets/). ## Perks, Skills, and Progression

Your perk build is just as important as your weapon choice. The **Responder** class focuses on healing, **Gene Therapy**, and infection resistance, making it the best choice for support players on Beaulieu Hospital and the Flooded map. The **Phalanx** class is a heavy gunner with increased damage resistance and ammo capacity, ideal for the Government Bunker and Night of the Living Dead. Spending **Merits** wisely across **no more room in hell 2 all maps** is the most efficient way to improve your survivability, as **Gene Therapy** upgrades carry over between matches.

### Responder Build: The Combat Medic

The **Responder** build prioritizes the **CRC** medical skill tree. Key perks include increased healing speed, reduced **NPV infection** spread, and a passive health regeneration. This build shines on Beaulieu Hospital, where the team takes constant chip damage from **Shambler** swarms. Pair it with the **Fierra DB12** for self-defense and a **Gruber 922** for precision shots on **Runners**. The **Responder** build also synergizes with the **Gene Therapy** stamina upgrades, allowing you to sprint between the hospital's three wings without exhausting yourself.

### Phalanx Build: The Heavy Gunner

The **Phalanx** build is all about raw firepower. Perks include increased ammo capacity, reduced recoil, and a damage bonus against **Walkers**. This build is mandatory for the Government Bunker's final stand and the Lighthouse's tower defense. Carry the **M7A1** as your primary and the **Sledgehammer** as your backup. The **Phalanx** build also benefits from **Gene Therapy** health upgrades, making you the last line of defense when the team is overwhelmed. On the **Nightmare** difficulty, a well-built **Phalanx** can hold a chokepoint solo while the **Responder** revives fallen teammates.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Which map is the easiest for beginners in No More Room in Hell 2?**

Beaulieu Hospital is the easiest map because it has wide hallways, generous weapon spawns, and a straightforward extraction objective. New players should learn the **Responder** class here before attempting the Government Bunker or Raven Rock.

**What is the best weapon for the no more room in hell 2 bunker map?**

The **Fierra DB12** shotgun is the best weapon for the Government Bunker because its tight corridors make close-range engagements unavoidable. Pair it with a **Sledgehammer** for melee backup and conserve **Credits** for medical supplies.

**How do I unlock the Night of the Living Dead map?**

The Night of the Living Dead map is unlocked by completing the Raven Rock scenario on any difficulty. It is also available in the custom game browser under the **Nightmare** difficulty modifier.

**What is the difference between Credits and Merits in NMRIH2?**

**Credits** are earned during a match and spent on ammunition, medical supplies, and weapon upgrades. **Merits** are a permanent currency earned by completing objectives and are used to unlock **Gene Therapy** upgrades and new perk slots.

**Is Permadeath mode available on all maps?**

Yes, **Permadeath** mode is available on every map in the rotation. In this mode, death is permanent, and your character's **Gene Therapy** upgrades are lost. It is the ultimate test of mastery for **no more room in hell 2 all maps**.
