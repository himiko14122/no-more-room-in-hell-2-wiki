## Understanding Raven Rock's Three-Phase Structure

Raven Rock is unique among the **no more room in hell 2 maps** because it is the only objective map that begins with a stealth segment. The other five objective maps — Lewiston, Broadway, Powerplant, Beaulieu Hospital, and the Bunker — all start with immediate combat. Raven Rock, however, drops your squad at the perimeter fence with a warning: the exterior patrols are dense, and alerting them early triggers a massive horde response that can overwhelm you before you even reach the main entrance.

### Phase One: Perimeter Fence Stealth Entry

The first phase of no more room in hell 2 raven rock secrets involves navigating the outer fence line without triggering alarms. The fence has three entry points: a main gate (heavily watched), a maintenance hatch (requires a **keycard** found in a nearby guard shack), and a collapsed wall section (risky, as it is exposed to a **Prime Zombie** patrol route). Most successful squads split into two groups — one draws attention at the main gate while the other slips through the maintenance hatch. This is one of the few **no more room in hell 2 map tips** that applies specifically to Raven Rock and not the other objective maps.

The guard shack near the maintenance hatch contains a guaranteed **M7A1** rifle spawn and a small medkit. It is one of the most valuable early **no more room in hell 2 supply locations** on the entire map. Grab it before entering the bunker. The stealth phase lasts roughly 90 seconds if executed cleanly. If your squad triggers an alarm, expect a wave of 15-20 **Runners** to converge on your position within 30 seconds. Surviving that wave is possible, but it burns ammunition and medical supplies you will need later.

### Phase Two: Interior Clearing and Keycard Hunt

Once inside, the map opens into the mess hall — a large communal area with multiple entrances. This is where the first major horde fight occurs. The mess hall has four doorways, and zombies spawn from all of them in a scripted sequence. Position your squad with two players covering each doorway. The **Fierra DB12** shotgun excels here because its spread handles the tight grouping of **Shamblers** that push through the doors.

After clearing the mess hall, the objective shifts to the server room. This is the keycard puzzle section. Your squad must locate three keycards scattered across the bunker: one in the kitchen cold storage, one in the barracks sleeping quarters, and one in the underground tunnels. Each keycard location has its own mini-encounter. The kitchen cold storage spawns a **Crawler** ambush behind the shelving units. The barracks have a **Walker** pack of about 12. The underground tunnels are the most dangerous — they contain a **Prime Zombie** that patrols a fixed route.

The underground tunnels are also home to some of the best hidden **no more room in hell 2 supply locations** on the map. A locked supply room in the tunnel network contains a **Sledgehammer**, two **Phalanx Pills**, and a full medkit. The key to this room is on a shelf in the server room itself, so grab it before starting the keycard hunt. This is one of the no more room in hell 2 raven rock secrets that most guides miss because it requires backtracking.

### Phase Three: Helipad Final Defense

The final phase triggers once all three keycards are inserted into the server room console. A 60-second countdown begins, and then the extraction helicopter arrives at the helipad on the roof. The path from the server room to the helipad is a straight corridor with two chokepoints. Zombies spawn in escalating waves during the transit, culminating in a massive horde at the helipad itself.

The helipad defense lasts 120 seconds. Your squad must hold a circular area with limited cover. The **Gruber 922** pistol is surprisingly effective here for conserving ammunition — headshots on **Shamblers** drop them in two rounds. The helicopter lands at the center of the pad, and extraction requires all living players to reach the boarding zone. If a player dies during the final defense, they cannot be revived — **Permadeath** is active on Raven Rock just like all other objective maps.

## Optimal Loadouts and Equipment Synergies

Your loadout choices matter more on Raven Rock than on any other map in the **nmrih2 map list**. The **M7A1** is the best all-around primary for Raven Rock. It has a 30-round magazine, moderate recoil, and enough penetration to hit multiple **Shamblers** in a line. The **Fierra DB12** shotgun is the second-best choice, particularly for the mess hall and helipad defense where zombies cluster. The **Sledgehammer** is a viable primary for players who want to conserve ammunition — it one-shots **Crawlers** and two-shots **Walkers**, but leaves you vulnerable during the swing animation.

**Phalanx Pills** are the most important consumable on Raven Rock. They delay **NPV infection** progression by 45 seconds per pill, which is critical in a map where you cannot easily retreat to a safe room. Stockpile both items in the server room before triggering the final extraction sequence.

**No more room in hell 2 raven rock secrets** also include knowing when to use **Credits** and **Merits**. Merits are better saved for unlocking permanent weapon attachments in the loadout screen before the run starts.

## Advanced Tactics and Squad Coordination

Raven Rock is the most coordination-heavy map in the **nmrih2 every map** rotation. Eight players who do not communicate will fail. The no more room in hell 2 raven rock secrets that separate successful squads from wipes are all about role assignment and timing.

### Role Assignment Strategy

Assign specific roles before the run begins. The recommended squad composition for Raven Rock is:

- **2 Point Players**: Carry the **Sledgehammer** and **Fierra DB12**, clear the initial stealth entry and lead through the mess hall
- **2 Support Players**: Carry **M7A1** rifles, cover the rear, and manage **Phalanx Pills** distribution
- **2 Scavengers**: Responsible for the Underground Tunnel detour, equipped with **Gruber 922** pistols for silent takedowns
- **2 Medics**: Carry all medical supplies, focus on keeping the squad's **NPV infection** in check

The Scavengers should break off from the main group immediately after the mess hall is cleared. They detour to the Underground Tunnel Supply Room while the other six players head toward the barracks and kitchen for the other two keycards. This parallel route saves roughly 4-5 minutes of run time and ensures the squad is fully stocked before the server room puzzle.

### Timing and Spawn Awareness

Zombie spawns on Raven Rock follow a scripted pattern, not a random one. The mess hall horde triggers when the last player crosses the threshold. The kitchen cold storage **Crawler** ambush triggers when the keycard is picked up. The barracks **Walker** pack spawns 10 seconds after the door is opened. Knowing these timings lets you prepare grenades and position your squad before the spawn triggers.

The **Prime Zombie** in the underground tunnels follows a fixed patrol route that takes 45 seconds to complete. If your Scavengers time their entry correctly, they can reach the Supply Room while the **Prime Zombie** is at the far end of its route. This is one of the most valuable no more room in hell 2 raven rock secrets because it eliminates the need to fight the **Prime Zombie** entirely.

### Communication Protocols

Use the in-game ping system to mark keycard locations and supply caches. The **extraction points** on Raven Rock are marked on the HUD, but the supply locations are not. A single ping on the Underground Tunnel Supply Room door saves your Scavengers from searching the entire tunnel network. Similarly, ping the **Phalanx Pills** when you find them so the Medics know where to direct injured players.

## Common Mistakes and How to Avoid Them

Even experienced squads make mistakes on Raven Rock. The no more room in hell 2 raven rock secrets are worthless if you fall into the same traps that wipe other teams. Here are the most common errors and their solutions.

### Splitting the Squad Too Early

The stealth phase tempts players to split into four groups of two to cover all three fence entry points simultaneously. This is a mistake. If one group triggers an alarm, the others are too far away to help. Keep the squad in two groups of four. The main gate group draws attention while the maintenance hatch group enters. Once inside, regroup in the mess hall before proceeding.

### Ignoring the Underground Tunnel Detour

Many squads skip the Underground Tunnel Supply Room because it requires fighting past the **Prime Zombie**. This is a critical error. The **Sledgehammer** and **Phalanx Pills** in that room are run-defining items. Without the **Sledgehammer**, your squad will burn through ammunition faster during the helipad defense. Without the extra **Phalanx Pills**, **NPV infection** will spread faster than your Medics can treat it.

### Wasting Ammo on Shamblers

**Shamblers** are slow and easy to dodge. Do not waste rifle rounds on them. Use the **Gruber 922** pistol or the **Sledgehammer** for **Shamblers**, and save your **M7A1** ammunition for **Runners** and **Prime Zombies** that actually threaten your squad. A squad that conserves ammunition through the interior phases will have plenty of firepower for the helipad defense.

### Triggering the Helipad Defense Understocked

The helipad defense is the hardest part of Raven Rock. If you trigger it with low health, high **NPV infection**, and minimal ammunition, you will fail. Before inserting the final keycard, have the entire squad gather in the server room. Distribute **Phalanx Pills**, apply **Gene Therapy** if available, and check everyone's ammunition. Only then should you trigger the countdown.
